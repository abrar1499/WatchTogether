    TOP SECRET · MISSION 01

    Operation Dust Signal

    Agent Kestrel, an enemy relay station at Hadra Flats is broadcasting stolen satellite codes. You go in alone. Get inside the base, copy the codes from the terminal in the command bunker, and walk out to helicopter landing zone Raven, north-west of the base.

    The guards carry rifles and they shoot well. If one of them sees you, the alarm goes off and soldiers come out of the barracks. The fence on the west side is broken. Use your binoculars before you move, and stay low.
